### Case Study: NHS Cyberattack
A big example of why security matters a lot is the NHS Cyberattack which happened in 2025. The NHS has a lot of confidential data that they cannot afford to lose since it includes patient records, their birth date, and any private data about them: name, address and national insurance numbers.

### The Impact on the People, Companies, and Society
* **What it did to Individuals:** Millions of patients had their private medical and personal data stolen.
* **The aftermath for businesses:** Pharmacies, hospitals, and clinics lost access to a lot of systems and even for the NHS directly took a hit since one of the deaths was directly linked to the cyberattack.
* **Bigger picture for society:** It can show how even the biggest corporation even government corporation which should be the most protected or seems most protected isn't and if such cyberattacks can happen to such big corporations then people should be careful what can happen to them as individuals.

### The Responsibility in Tech You should take 
When building any sort of system, especially when it is IT related and has a connection to the internet you should be making sure at every stage the data will never be intercepted by anyone it shouldn't be.
To prevent this:
* Make sure all systems are updated as most updates include security patches.
* Ensure all data is backed and well encrypted so no one that shouldn't have access doesn't have it.

### Professional Behaviour & Company Values
For me, acting like a real IT professional comes down to how YOU handle yourself on the job:
* **Staying Calm Under Pressure:** When systems break or things get stressful, panicking doesn't fix anything. You have to stay calm and focus on the fix.
* **Getting On With the Work:** Make sure you meet the deadlines that the work is due for, and that it is done with quality.
* **Respect & Fairness:** Treating everyone the exact same way as a colleague, no matter who they are and at what skill level, and just make sure that you help everyone that they need it.
